# Prospective wall-time follow-up

Written October 15, 2026, after the update-budget diagnostic. That development round found a positive original-substrate mean-error comparison on some familiar families at 960 updates, while its measured NumPy training time was higher. This follow-up tests an implementation-specific equal-elapsed-training-time comparison on fresh generated systems; it is not a hardware-normalized or confirmatory architecture benchmark.

Freeze source/configuration before execution. Use system seeds 701,809,907, the same three generator families, 32 training trajectories of eight intervals, eight validation trajectories of twelve intervals, and sixteen held-out trajectories of forty-eight intervals. Use the same q=8, four hidden states, exact initial states, known inputs, variance floor, noiseless targets, and integrator as earlier.

Four models: original 736-parameter gates, 820-parameter competing states, and respective 730-/808-parameter neural controls. Each gets three learning-rate trials (0.003,0.01,0.03) and 1.5 seconds of elapsed training/selection time per trial. The curriculum switches horizons 2/4/8 at elapsed one-third/two-thirds of the budget. Check validation every twenty updates and once at the end. Include validation time; allow one update/check overshoot and report actual time and steps. No warmup is excluded. Run models sequentially without heavy parallel work. Operating-system scheduling and hardware load can affect the outcome; this is a diagnostic of this implementation, not a general algorithmic speed ranking.

Select checkpoint and learning rate by validation nMSE, then evaluate the forty-eight-interval held-out rollout. Faster implementations receive more optimizer updates and validation checks by design; count and report both. A win from more steps does not isolate a representational cause. Include the affine model with its separately measured closed-form fitting time as context, not as an equal-budget neural trial.

These are eighteen new/fresh family-system cases across the two diagnostic rounds combined; within this round there are nine systems. Matching seeds across functional families pair some generator parameters, so no pooled confidence claim is appropriate. Do not retune after viewing this follow-up, reuse old held-out seeds as confirmation, or describe an internal development comparison as a world benchmark.
