# Source ledger

Search date: October 15, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. References are grouped by mechanism, not ranked by citation count. A paper's existence is evidence against a broad novelty claim; it is not independent replication of its results.

Access codes: **F** = relevant full-text sections inspected; **A** = primary abstract/record inspected; **E** = indexed primary-paper excerpts inspected, with direct retrieval unavailable or not completed. These codes do not imply every supplement was read. Some PMC/PubMed pages returned a challenge or empty page on opening even when their indexed content was available. Publisher 403 responses are explicitly not full-text access.

**S42. Heistinger et al., Plasticity of genetic regulation during cellular learning**, bioRxiv preprint (2026), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.27.728101). Existence and title confirmed through the [authors' ETH page](https://bc.biol.ethz.ch/research/barral/research-barral/adaptive-decision-making-in-budding-yeast.html) and the bioRxiv monthly archive. This record prevents the older papers from being mistaken for the complete current literature.

## Limits of this ledger

An abstract-level source supports only abstract-level conclusions. Several candidates received a preliminary screen rather than a full reproduction audit. The leading computational comparators received deeper methods inspection, but unpublished work, inaccessible supplements, non-English literature, patents, and unindexed software remain possible sources of overlap. None of the access failures constitutes evidence that a method or result does not exist.
